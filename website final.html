About 5paisa Capital Limited

2007Incorporated
2016Digital discount brokerage launched
Nov 2017Separately listed company
ThaneHeadquartered in Maharashtra

5paisa Capital Limited is an established Indian financial technology and stockbroking company, originally incorporated in 2007. The company launched its digital discount brokerage operations in 2016. It was initially associated with the IIFL Group before becoming an independent entity. Following its demerger, 5paisa became a separately listed company in November 2017. Headquartered in Thane, Maharashtra, it serves retail investors and active traders across India.

The company provides online access to equity trading, futures and options, and commodity markets. It also offers mutual funds, SIP investments, IPO applications and Demat account services.

5paisa is a registered stockbroker with memberships across major Indian stock exchanges. Its technology-driven platform provides digital trading facilities, research tools and investment solutions. Today, 5paisa continues to contribute to India's fintech ecosystem by making online trading and investing more accessible.
